// Client Project

FyndIt

A platform for finding verified, trusted businesses across South Africa.

FyndIt is a full-stack web application developed to streamline the discovery and management of verified businesses across South Africa. The platform combines location-based search, business management, vendor verification, administrative tools and SEO functionality.
